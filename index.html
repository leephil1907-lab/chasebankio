Before you explore

Frequently asked
questions.

Is Harborline a real bank?

No. Harborline is a fictional brand used for a front-end banking experience prototype. It is not affiliated with Chase or JPMorgan Chase.

Does sign-in use real authentication?

No. The sign-in and dashboard are a demonstration flow using fictional sample data. Never enter a real password or sensitive financial information.

Can I open an actual account or apply for a card?

No. Forms only demonstrate user-interface behavior and do not submit applications, check eligibility or create financial accounts.
